COOPER CHEF

Support

A little help for your pretend kitchen. Cooper Chef is a cooking game for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.

Contact support

Parents and carers can email SUPPORT_EMAIL for help, feedback, or privacy questions. Cooper Chef is provided by Tien Dao.

Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Please avoid sending children’s names, photos, voice recordings, or other sensitive information. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information first.

Start cooking

Choose pizza, ice cream, fruit plate, fruit basket, or sandwiches. Tap an ingredient in the tray, then tap the canvas to place it. Tap again to add more. Drag a creation to move it, pinch to zoom, and use Undo or the eraser to make changes. Pizza has a pretend oven step; other dishes can be served directly.

Grown-up settings

Open the grown-up settings and answer the arithmetic question. These controls let you create dishes, customise ingredient and container photos, change the kitchen background, and personalise spoken feedback. The arithmetic prompt helps prevent accidental access; it is not a password or secure lock.

Common questions

Does Cooper Chef need the internet or an account?
No. Cooking and customisation work without an account or app server. Selecting a file or photo stored in a cloud service may require that service to download it.
Why can’t I take a photo or record audio?
Allow Camera or Microphone access in the device Settings for Cooper Chef. Permissions are optional and only needed for the corresponding customisation feature. You can choose a photo or import an audio file instead. Camera capture requires a device with a camera.
Why is there no sound?
Check the app’s speaker toggle, device volume, silent mode, and Bluetooth or headphone output. In grown-up settings, check the selected feedback phrase or custom audio.
Why is my custom dish missing from the recipe list?
In My dishes, add a base photo and at least one ingredient. Every ingredient must have a photo before the dish is ready to play.
How do I move a recipe to another device?
In grown-up settings, open My dishes, select the dish, and export the recipe to a file. Transfer the file using your chosen service. On the other device, use My dishes → Import recipe from file. Recipes include names, photos, and sizes; feedback recordings and kitchen backgrounds are not included. Imported recipe files must be valid and no larger than 50 MB.
Why won’t an audio file import?
Choose an audio file supported by the device and no larger than 10 MB. Custom recordings can be up to 30 seconds long.
How do I remove saved content?
Use the removal controls for photos, custom dishes, ingredients, and feedback clips in grown-up settings. To clear all local app content and preferences, delete the app using Delete App, rather than Offload App. Export anything you want to keep first. Delete exported files and manage device backups separately.
